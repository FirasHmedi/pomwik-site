Method

How it works

At every step, you know what is happening and what you will pay.

  1. 01

    Free app check

    You describe the app, we tell you whether we take it.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    A clear report, the list of blockers and a fixed price.

  3. 03

    Access

    You invite us to your accounts (code, App Store, Google Play) with limited rights. We never ask for your passwords.

  4. 04

    Work on a test copy

    Never on your real users’ data.

  5. 05

    Publishing

    Apple and Google approve the app, then you pay the balance.

  6. 06

    Maintenance, optional

    Updates required by Apple and Google, and crash monitoring.

Scope

What we take, what we turn down

We’d rather tell you up front: some apps need a specialised team.

Yes

We take

  • Publishing on the App Store and Google Play.
  • Fixing apps built with Lovable or Bolt: security, data. Including with a Supabase database.
  • Recent mobile apps. Expo or React Native.
  • Turning a website into an app, on quote.
  • Rewriting on a solid base, on quote. In Expo.
No

We turn down

  • Health or banking apps.
  • Apps tied to a device: Bluetooth, augmented reality.
  • Apps built on outdated technology. Objective-C, Java, Cordova, React Native 0.70 or older.
  • Copies of existing apps.
  • Jobs that require working directly on your real data.

Questions

What people often ask

My app was built with Lovable. Can you help?

Yes, quote after the €99 assessment.

Do you guarantee approval?

The balance is only paid on approval. Rejections due to the app’s content or business model are excluded (see pricing). We tell you at the assessment if your app is at risk of this kind of rejection.

Who owns the code and the accounts?

You do. The app is published under your developer accounts, and the code belongs to you.

Do you need my passwords?

No. Only invitations with limited rights, which you can revoke at any time.

How long does it take?

Publishing takes 1 to 3 weeks, depending on store review. A takeover is priced at the assessment.

Do you work outside France?

Yes: Belgium, Switzerland, Luxembourg, and in English for the UK and Ireland.

Tell us about your app

A few lines are enough: what the app does, what it was built with, what is stuck. We reply within 12 hours.
