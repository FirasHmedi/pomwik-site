Privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Version française

This page explains what Pomwik does with what you send us through this site: the free check form on the Realm® pages, the contact form, emails you send us, and the app waitlists.

Who is responsible

Pomwik. The publisher's details are in the legal notice (French).

Free Realm check and contact form

The form sends us the name, email address and message you type, plus the language and address of the page you sent it from. It goes to our server at Cloudflare, which emails it to our inbox at Google (Gmail). Our server does not store your name, email address or message.

You then get one automatic email that confirms we received your message. Its text is fixed and contains nothing you typed. It is sent through Resend or through our Gmail account.

To limit abuse, our server keeps a log of each message: the time, a fingerprint of your IP address that changes every day (a hash of the address and the date), and whether our two emails were sent. It uses this log to accept at most 5 messages per hour from one connection. Entries older than 30 days are deleted each time a message is sent through one of our contact forms.

We use what you send only to reply to you and to prepare what you ask for, before any contract. We keep these emails for 3 years after our last exchange, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. The same applies if you email us directly.

App waitlists

When you join the waitlist of Petty, Fieldwrite or Settled, we store your email address, the app you chose, your answers to the optional questions on the form, and the date and time you signed up. We don't store your IP address with your signup.

We use your address only to email you about the app you chose: one welcome email confirming you joined, and one email when it's ready to try. We also get a notification of each signup so we can see how much interest there is. We won't use your address for other apps you didn't sign up for.

Legal basis: your consent. By pressing "Join the waitlist" you agree to receive emails about that app. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

Signups are stored in a database at Cloudflare. We keep them until you ask us to remove them, or until 24 months after you sign up if the app hasn't launched by then, whichever comes first.

Service providers

Cloudflare (our server, database, email routing and audience measurement), Google (the Gmail inbox where messages arrive, which can also send our automatic emails), Resend (automatic emails) and GitHub (hosting of this site, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses). Some of them process data outside the European Union. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Audience measurement

To count visits, the site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies or identifiers: pages viewed, country, browser and load time. No visit is linked to a person. The site uses no advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your data, or to stop emailing you, at any time. Every waitlist email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email hello@pomwik.com from the address you used and we'll act on it. If you're in the EU or UK you can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Our waitlists are for adults. Please don't sign up if you're under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above.
